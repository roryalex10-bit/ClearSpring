Seven weeks to recognise your saboteurs and strengthen your sage

Your saboteurs are the familiar inner voices that take over under pressure: the harsh critic, the worrier, the one who can't say no. Your sage is the calmer, wiser part of you that meets the same moments with curiosity and clarity. This programme helps you notice the first and strengthen the second.

  1. Discover

    Take the free assessment to see which saboteurs run the show for you.

  2. Learn

    Short weekly videos and daily audio themes, at your own pace.

  3. Practise

    Daily "PQ reps": brief mindfulness exercises that build new mental muscle.

  4. Connect

    Weekly pod calls with a small group, held with warmth rather than pressure.

Hello, I'm Becky

I'm an NHS GP in York with over 25 years in primary care, and I'm also a GP trainer, appraiser, coach and mentor.

I've spent my career alongside people through some of the biggest moments in their lives. I've also had my own share of challenges: juggling the intensity of work, home and relationships, and stretching myself beyond my comfort zone in leadership roles. A coaching approach has been invaluable to me through all of it.

I'm endlessly curious about what helps us truly flourish, and I love helping others create lives that feel vibrant, authentic, fulfilling and joyful.

I first trained as a coach with Blue Sky Coaching in 2009, and have since trained in Clean Language coaching and Positive Intelligence coaching. I've been particularly inspired by Tara Brach's teaching on mindfulness and radical self-compassion, and by the Positive Intelligence programme for mental fitness, which is a great ‘operating system’ to apply to every part of life. These practices are part of my own daily life, and I bring what I've learned into my coaching.

We all build patterns to protect ourselves from fear and self-doubt, and it's easy to end up living from those patterns on autopilot. Once we learn to notice them, we can pause and choose to respond from a calmer, wiser and kinder place.

Insight alone isn't enough, though. It's small, regular practice that rewires the brain and makes this our new normal, and it's so much easier with the support of others. From this more empowered place, life starts to feel different: more ease, more confidence, more joy, and real progress towards what matters most. It's been a game changer for me and many of my clients.

I work with GPs, healthcare teams and people from all walks of life who are capable, committed and ready for something to shift.

When I'm not working, you'll find me hiking, running, doing yoga or planning the next adventure.

If any of this resonates and you'd like to explore further, I'd love to hear from you.
