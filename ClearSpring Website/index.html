Seven weeks to recognise your saboteurs and strengthen your sage

Your saboteurs are the familiar inner voices that take over under pressure: the harsh critic, the worrier, the one who can't say no. Your sage is the calmer, wiser part of you that meets the same moments with curiosity and clarity. This programme helps you notice the first and strengthen the second.

  1. Discover

    Take the free assessment to see which saboteurs run the show for you.

  2. Learn

    Short weekly videos and daily audio themes, at your own pace.

  3. Practise

    Daily "PQ reps": brief mindfulness exercises that build new mental muscle.

  4. Connect

    Weekly pod calls with a small group, held with warmth rather than pressure.

Hello, I'm Becky

I'm a GP and coach based in York. After years of watching brilliant, capable people (colleagues, clients, friends) run themselves into the ground on autopilot, I trained in Positive Intelligence to find a gentler way through.

Clearspring blends that framework with the Chimp Paradox, the Enneagram and neuroscience. Not to fix you, but to help you find your way back to the clarity that's already there.
